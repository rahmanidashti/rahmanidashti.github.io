This blog post is inspired by Hamed's earlier blog posts for SIGIR reports.

Unfortunately, I did not have the chance to attend SIGIR 2023 this year in Taiwan. I was mostly following the updates from SIGIR on Twitter — thanks to everyone who was sharing the amazing and inspiring moments at SIGIR 2023, and congratulations to all the winners!

However, I thought it would be great to have a very short stat of SIGIR this year. This is the first time that I post the state of a conference like SIGIR. During the past years, Hamed Zamani and Jimmy Lin reported the stats of SIGIR conferences: SIGIR 2019, SIGIR 2020, and SIGIR 2021.

The following information and summarisation have been automatically extracted from the full list of accepted papers of all tracks at ACM SIGIR 2023. Please let me know if you find any errors in the reported statistics. Note that the SIGIR website only released the title and the names of the authors of the accepted papers, so there is no affiliation/institution-level analysis in this report.

First, we can take a look at a WordCloud created from the titles of the accepted papers (all tracks, excluding Doctoral Consortium papers):

The WordCloud of the titles of all papers shows a high frequency of words such as Recommendation, Learning, Search, Retrieval, User, Text, and Graph, which is not very surprising as we have seen a similar pattern in the SIGIR 2021 report.

The WordCloud of (a) Full, (b) SIRIP, and (c) Doctoral Consortium papers.

We can see the WordCloud for full (165 papers), SIRIP-Industry (40 papers), and Doctoral Consortium (11 papers) papers. There is a higher frequency of the words Graph and Learning in full papers, while they are not very frequent in SIRIP papers. However, we see the word Online, which indicates the importance of online experiments and evaluation. More interestingly, doctoral consortium papers show a high frequency of the words Large and Augmentation, indicating a high prevalence of LLM-related PhD topics.
