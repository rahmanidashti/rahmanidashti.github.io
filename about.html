About

I am a third-year PhD student in Computer Science at University College London, affiliated with the Web Intelligence Group at the UCL Centre for Artificial Intelligence, and a PhD Enrichment Student at The Alan Turing Institute. I am advised by Prof. Emine Yilmaz and Nick Craswell.

Before UCL I completed an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Zanjan, advised by Prof. Mohsen Afsharchi and Dr. Mitra Baratchi of Leiden University, where I worked on context-aware recommender systems. My earliest research was in bioinformatics — analysing protein interaction networks to distinguish cancerous from normal cells.

My work today sits at the intersection of information retrieval and large language models: how we evaluate retrieval and generation systems, whether LLMs can stand in for human assessors, and how synthetic data can make evaluation cheaper without making it less trustworthy. Alongside that I care about fairness in recommendation and about clarification in conversational search — knowing when a system should ask rather than answer.
